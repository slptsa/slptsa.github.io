## Cookies for Our Wonderful Staff

Our teachers and staff pour their hearts into our kids every day, and the PTSA wants to thank them with a holiday treat party! Please send in something for the staff to share and enjoy. Homemade or store-bought, it's all appreciated.

Drop off treats at the school office:

- **Monday, December 14** (after school), or
- **Tuesday, December 15** (before school)

Let's fill the staff room with cookies and show the SLE team how much they mean to us!

## Thank You for Running 2 Be Kind!

Together, our Run 2 Be Kind raised **over $7,000** for school programs. Thank you to every runner, cheerleader, and donor! To celebrate, students danced the recess away with Ollie the Otter on Friday, November 6th.

Missed your chance to give? It's not too late! You can still [make a donation online](https://www.givebacks.com/causes/slptsa/campaigns/dbce996c9087).
